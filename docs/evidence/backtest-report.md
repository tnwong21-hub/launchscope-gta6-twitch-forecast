# RAMQE Backtest Report — LaunchScope (local research, not an official grade)

Generated by `repo/tools/backtest.ts` from the official dev-kit comparables. **No practice target
answers exist in the kit; nothing here is an official make.fun score.** The four practice launches
(p00–p03) are withheld from the comparables list and their targets are never used.

## Protocol — leave-one-comparable-out (LOCO)
- 37 usable comparable launches (the 38th, the GTA6 prelaunch category, has no release-day hour-1
  and is excluded from ratio curves).
- For each launch `c`: cut its own curve at its hour 1 (hide hours 2–24), forecast from the **other
  36** comparables only, then score hours 2–24 against `c`'s actual viewers.
- Exact competition loss: mean over all (hour, level) items of `2*max(tau*d, (tau-1)*d)`,
  `d = ln(y) - ln(q)`. Also reports typical miss % and 90% coverage.
- "Rolling subset" = 11 clean rolling-midnight console launches — the regime that matches all four
  practice launches and the GTA6 final. "All clean" = 26 tier-`comparable` launches.

## Headline result

| Forecast | Loss (all 37) | Miss % | Cov90 | **Loss (rolling 11)** | Miss % | Cov90 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| House baseline (persistence) | 0.929650 | 213.3 | 0.624 | 0.363046 | 73.6 | 0.802 |
| **RAMQE (production config)** | **0.395114** | 82.1 | 0.908 | **0.251989** | 43.2 | 0.901 |

On the target-relevant rolling-midnight regime, RAMQE reduces log pinball loss by ~31% versus the
house baseline and cuts typical miss from 73.6% to 43.2%, with 90% coverage essentially at the
nominal 0.90. The all-37 aggregate is dominated by late-unlock/early-access comparables whose hour-1
is pre-surge (e.g. Silksong ~2120×, Black Myth Wukong ~92× hour-1); those are deliberately
down-weighted for a rolling target and are not representative of the final case.

## Band-width calibration
Rolling-subset width grid (in-sample on comparables, reported transparently):

| multiplier | 0.25 | 0.40 | 0.60 | 0.80 | 1.00 | 1.25 | 1.50 | 2.00 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| loss | 0.2780 | 0.2580 | 0.2480 | 0.2476 | 0.2520 | 0.2643 | 0.2827 | 0.3217 |
| cov90 | 0.490 | 0.668 | 0.818 | 0.866 | 0.901 | 0.937 | 0.972 | 1.000 |

Production keeps the multiplier at **1.00**: although 0.80 is marginally lower loss, 1.00 lands
coverage at the nominal 0.90, which is the honest, non-overfit choice for a proper scoring rule.
Scale-bandwidth sweep (0.8 / 1.2 / 1.6 / 2.2) gave rolling losses 0.2435 / 0.2520 / 0.2698 /
0.2960; the middle value 1.2 is kept to avoid overfitting an 11-launch subset.

Resident Evil Requiem is the single large failure: its 00:00Z hour-1 is EU-only before the US
unlock, giving a 12× rise that the rolling-consensus median cannot anticipate. GTA6's hour-1 is
expected to include NZ, central Europe and the UK before the US unlock, i.e. a higher starting
fraction — closer to the 1.3–3.6× cluster that dominates the subset. This is an assumption, not a
guarantee; the model's calibrated band is deliberately wide enough to include such regimes.

## Limitations
- Tuning is in-sample on the 38 comparables; no official practice or final grade is available.
- Only 11 clean rolling comparables support the regime-specific numbers — treat them as indicative.
- The final uses minute-mean prints, practice uses SullyGnome hourly averages; the measures differ.
- The final case will contain the four practice launches as extra comparables (42 total); the model
  will use them automatically by regime/tier/scale, but they cannot be validated locally.
